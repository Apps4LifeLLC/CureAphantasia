# Thinking Styles

• **Verbal Thinking** - Thoughts which are structured based on language and reasoning. Facilitated by the Inner Monologue. Often the default style for practitioners; must be actively quieted to permit Sensory Thinking during visualization practice.

• **Sensory Thinking** - Thoughts which are structured based on sensorial processes (ie Sight, Taste, Touch, Sound, Smell). The foundational substrate of all visualization work. Recall means reproducing sense data directly, not describing it — labeling ("it's red") collapses back into Verbal Thinking.

• **Conceptual Thinking** - Thoughts which are structured based on latent platonic properties (eg Spatial, Orientation, Motion, etc). Sometimes called _analogue thinking_ when it substitutes for Sensory Thinking — _knowing_ an apple is red without _seeing_ it red. High analogue profiles correlate anecdotally with increased Dissociation.

• **Emotional Thinking** - Thoughts which are structured based on emotional feelings. Affect as the carrier of meaning rather than words or images. Amplifies vividness and persistence when entangled with Sensory Thinking; suppressed or avoided emotions have been proposed as a driver of acquired Aphantasia.

# Mental Mechanisms

• **Inner Monologue** - A Mental Mechanism for facilitating Verbal Thinking. The Inner Monologue primarily facilitates verbal thoughts, and can synergize with Sensory Thinking (specifically _Sound Sensory Thinking_) to attach voices to verbal thoughts (pitch, timbre, prosody). The Inner Monologue can have parallel instances (ie '_Inner Dialogue_'), enabling simulated conversations and the interaction modes underlying Tulpa practice.

• **Minds Eye** - The felt locus where visual imagery appears; a sub-component of the `Theater of the Mind`. Practitioners disagree about _where_ it is located (behind the forehead, inside the skull, in front of the face, non-spatial); early-stage learners often report fragmented or peripheral images, and one training goal is bringing imagery to a stable central location. Attention inside the Minds Eye (_mental gaze_) is decoupleable from ocular gaze — the eyes can move freely while the mental gaze holds on a stable mental object.

• **Theater of the Mind** - The broader inner stage where simulated sensory experience plays out — the multi-modal container of which the Minds Eye is the visual component. Holds not just images but sound (see `Auricular Sound`), tactile sensation, motion, spatial layout, and emotional atmosphere. Full-scene persistence (walking through a memory house, inhabiting a first-person environment) is the training of the Theater rather than of isolated images.

# Mental Processes

• **Imposition** - Placing mental imagery into a specific location in perceived space such that it integrates with the physical scene. An imposed object respects scene geometry — it sits on the actually-present chair, occludes objects behind it, casts a plausible apparent shadow, and persists in its location as the practitioner moves their head. Advanced Tulpa practitioners treat imposition as the tangibility step: the tulpa stops being a Minds-Eye image and starts appearing present in the room. _Terminology may change ['warping' vs 'projecting' maybe?]_

• **Projection** - Casting mental imagery outward into the visual field as a super-imposition — "seeing at your thoughts" rather than seeing them internally. The core operation of Prophantasia. Typically viewer-locked (moves with the head/gaze) and often 2D or screen-like in early stages; controlled projection requires learning to _stop_ as well as to _start_.

Rough convention: **imposition integrates with scene geometry; projection overlays the visual field.** The boundary is considered unsettled.

# Modalities

• **Auricular Sound** - Mental generation, playback, and manipulation of auditory imagery — hearing sounds, music, voices, and acoustic environments _in mind_. The auditory analogue of the Phantasia Spectrum. _Mind's ear_ is the auditory counterpart to the Minds Eye. Distinct from Inner Monologue: monologue carries propositional content via a voice; Auricular Sound is the full mental rendering of sound as a sound object regardless of semantic content.

• **3D Sight** - A perceptual filter applied to incoming visual (and sometimes auditory) stimuli such that flat media (video, screens, GIFs) and low-depth environments are experienced as genuinely three-dimensional, immersive, and first-person-present. Modifies _incoming_ perception rather than projecting _internal_ imagery — distinct from Prophantasia, but often potentiates Imposition by sharpening the depth substrate imposed objects live in.

# States

• **Dissociation** - Disconnection between mental awareness and sensory/physical reality. Correlated with Aphantasia and over-reliance on Verbal + Conceptual Thinking at the expense of Sensory Thinking. Non-clinical community usage — a spectrum from mild derealization to full depersonalization, distinct from DID. As sensory imagination capacity increases, dissociation is commonly reported to decrease.

• **Tulpa** - A constructed sentient-feeling entity within one mind — a self-aware companion developed through sustained attention, personality definition, and habitual interaction. Framed as an _identity shift_ within a single mind, not a separate consciousness. Explicitly _not_ equivalent to DID (trauma-based, non-interactive) or psychotic hallucination.

• **Tetris Effect** - Involuntary intrusion of visual, behavioral, and thought patterns following prolonged exposure to a repetitive stimulus. Named for post-gaming Tetris imagery; generalizes to any sustained visual focus (Factorio belts, chess positions, mandalas). Occupies a distinct perceptual screen (low-contrast, motion-based, behind the eyelids). Shares space and mechanism with Hypnagogia; can serve as an entry point to voluntary Autogogia.
