# Glossary

One-line definitions of every term used in this repo. For deeper treatments, follow the links to [Dictionary](./Dictionary.md), [Phantasia Spectrum](./PhantasiaSpectrum.md), [Techniques](./Techniques.md), [Phenomena](./Phenomena.md), and [Comparisons](./Comparisons.md).

----

# Phantasia spectrum

• **Aphantasia** - No voluntary visual imagery.

• **Hypophantasia** - Weak, fragmented imagery ("hypophan").

• **Traditional Phantasia** - Stable internal imagery ("tradphan").

• **Prophantasia** - Imagery projected into the visual field ("prophan").

• **Hyperphantasia** - Imagery as vivid as or more vivid than perception.

• **Autogogia** - Waking hypnagogia; imagery on a screen between eyelids and visual noise.

• **Hypnagogia** - Spontaneous imagery at the sleep threshold.

• **Tulpa** - Constructed sentient-feeling inner entity.

• **Tetris Effect** - Involuntary imagery intrusion from prolonged repetitive stimulus.

----

# Thinking styles

• **Verbal Thinking** - Language-structured thought.

• **Sensory Thinking** - Sense-data-structured thought.

• **Conceptual Thinking** - Platonic / analogue thought (spatial, orientation, motion).

• **Emotional Thinking** - Affect-structured thought.

----

# Mental mechanisms

• **Inner Monologue** - Verbal-thinking apparatus; can have parallel instances.

• **Minds Eye** - Visual locus in the Theater of the Mind.

• **Theater of the Mind** - Multi-modal inner stage.

----

# Mental processes

• **Imposition** - Embedding mental imagery in 3D scene geometry.

• **Projection** - Overlaying mental imagery on the visual field.

----

# Modalities

• **3D Sight** - Perceptual filter rendering flat media as immersive 3D.

• **Auricular Sound** - Mental auditory imagination (auditory phantasia).

----

# States

• **Dissociation** - Derealization from under-use of sensory thinking.

----

# Phenomena

• **Palinopsia** - Visual persistence after stimulus removal.

• **Afterimages** - Residual visual signal; negative / positive / photographic / cognitive variants.

• **Phosphenes** - Spontaneous neural light patterns independent of retinal input.

• **Visual Snow** - Persistent granular baseline noise in the visual field.

• **Mental Gaze** - Attention locus inside mental space; decoupleable from ocular gaze.

• **Bandwidth** - Number of visual properties held simultaneously (also _cardinality_).

• **Persistence** - Duration imagery remains stable.

• **Fragmentation** - Imagery breaking into pieces; includes tunnel vision and kaleidoscopic instability.

• **Color Flooding** - Filling inner visual space with a single saturated color.

• **SDAM** - Severely Deficient Autobiographical Memory; co-occurs with Aphantasia.

----

# Techniques

• **Image Streaming** - Narrate emerging imagery aloud (classic + aphantasia-specific variants).

• **Kasina** - Single-object meditation; flame or colored disc.

• **Memory Palace** - Spatial-mnemonic walkthrough.

• **Flash Technique** - Afterimage-chain onramp for Prophantasia.

• **Rapid Expansion** - Traditional-phantasia bandwidth drill via fast focal-point changes.

• **Drawing Induction** - Autogogia onramp via mental sketching.

• **Pendulum Technique** - Constant-load background Traditional Phantasia training.

• **Dream Training** - WILD / DILD / WBTB / SATS / reality checks.

# Community and external terms

• **Analogue thinking** - Colloquial for Conceptual Thinking when it substitutes for Sensory Thinking.

• **Tradphan / prophan / hypophan / hyperphan** - Slang abbreviations.

• **Photographic afterimage** - True-color non-inverting afterimage; a Prophantasia training target.

• **Cone fatigue** - Retinal origin of illusory afterimages; distinguished from mental afterimages.

• **SATS (State Akin to Sleep)** - Neville-Goddard protocol; hold a visualization while dropping toward sleep.

• **WILD / DILD / WBTB** - Lucid-dreaming entry protocols.

• **Ganzflicker / Ganzfeld** - Flicker / sensory-deprivation apparatuses.

• **Psychonetics** - Oleg's advanced visualization / energy-work text.

• **RCCX / CYP21A2** - Genetic complex hypothesized as a visualization-impairment substrate.

• **HPPD** - Hallucinogen Persisting Perception Disorder; cautionary reference for aggressive Prophantasia / Autogogia training.

• **HSP** - Highly Sensitive Person; related sensory-processing profile.

• **Capacity / bandwidth / cardinality** - Interchangeable terms for the simultaneous-holding metric.

• **Mental gaze vs. ocular gaze** - Decoupleable attention locus vs. eye position.

• **"Seeing is thinking"** - Traditional Phantasia framing.

• **"Seeing at thoughts"** - Prophantasia framing.

• **r/CureAphantasia vs. r/Aphantasia** - Improvable-spectrum vs. identity-neutral community stances.
