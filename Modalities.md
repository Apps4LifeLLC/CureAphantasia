# Modalities

Non-visual channels of mental imagery. The Phantasia Spectrum is usually discussed in visual terms, but it has direct analogues in every sense. This document covers the two most developed in community practice — Auricular Sound (hearing) and 3D Sight (spatial perception).

----

# Auricular Sound

Mental generation, playback, and manipulation of auditory imagery — hearing sounds, music, voices, and acoustic environments _in mind_. The auditory analogue of the Phantasia Spectrum.

## Variants

• **Passive sound memory** - Recall of familiar sounds (songs, voices) without deliberate construction.

• **Active auricular imagination** - Voluntary generation of novel sounds — rearranging melodies, simulating voices saying new lines, imagining acoustic scenes never actually heard.

• **Auditory prophantasia** - Voluntary, sustained, detailed control over internal soundscapes. Pitch, timbre, rhythm, spatial position, and performance nuance all manipulable.

• **Pitch discrimination** - Accuracy of internal pitch recall. Related to but distinct from perfect / absolute pitch training.

## Terms

• **Mind's ear** - The auditory counterpart to the Minds Eye.

• **Binaural processing** - Spatial positioning of imagined sounds (left / right, near / far, elevation).

• **Multisensory linkage** - Sounds tend to stay semantically bound to their visual / kinesthetic context. Training often involves isolating pure audio from its anchor.

## Distinction from Inner Monologue

The Inner Monologue carries _propositional content_ via a voice. Auricular Sound is the full rendering of sound _as a sound object_ regardless of semantic content. A musician "hearing" a chord without words is using Auricular Sound; "hearing" the sentence "that's a minor chord" is using the Inner Monologue.

## Interference

Inner Monologue dominance can either obstruct or facilitate Auricular Sound depending on control. Training often requires learning to _stop_ internal narration of the sound and hold the pure auditory content.

## Techniques

• **Ear training apps** (e.g., Functional Ear Trainer) - Build pitch discrimination.

• **Diverse music listening** - Expands auditory memory breadth across genres and cultural traditions.

• **Binaural beats** - Used by some practitioners as an entry aid; reports vary.

• **White noise as imagery medium** - Analogous to visual Imposition onto visual noise; imagined sounds can be "placed" inside the noise texture.

• **Memorable source material** - Personal voice, movie quotes, specific music passages. Easier than random phonemes.

• **Altered-state facilitation** - Exhaustion, relaxation, immediately upon waking. Lowered internal-narration baseline.

# 3D Sight

A perceptual filter applied to incoming visual (and sometimes auditory) stimuli such that flat media (video, screens, GIFs) and low-depth environments are experienced as genuinely three-dimensional, immersive, and first-person-present.

## Distinction from Prophantasia and Imposition

3D Sight modifies _incoming_ perception. Prophantasia projects _internal_ imagery onto the visual field. Imposition places internal imagery _inside_ perceived 3D space.

3D Sight and Imposition are complementary: a sharper depth substrate gives imposed objects a more convincing place to live.

## Mechanics

• **Observer → participant frame-shift** - Deliberate choice to inhabit a scene rather than watch it from outside.

• **Sustained depth attention** - Parallax, occlusion, shadow. Gaze resting on depth cues rather than on the image surface.

• **Saccade reduction** - Minimizing eye movement stabilizes the 3D read; many practitioners report that rapid saccades collapse the effect.

• **Cross-modal extension** - Some practitioners report spatial audio emerging alongside — sounds positioned in perceived 3D space.

## Techniques

• **Magic-eye / stereograms** - Train the depth-fusion apparatus.

• **Vision yoga routines** - Intentional eye-muscle work; reports vary.

• **Dual-monitor clone mode** - Continuous 3D exposure used as ambient practice.

• **VR-perspective footage** - Watch first-person immersive video while mentally simulating the embodied motion (flying, diving, running).

• **Spontaneous development** - A few practitioners report it emerging after headstands or periods of intense image study; unclear whether causal.

## Relations

• Strongly linked to Imposition and Sensory Thinking.

• Complementary to Prophantasia rather than competing with it.

• Can deepen Emotional Thinking engagement via the sense of being _in_ the scene.

• Recovery from visual Dissociation often tracks with 3D Sight development.
